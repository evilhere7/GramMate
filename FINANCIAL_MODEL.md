# GramMate: Financial Model & Unit Economics
## Revenue Projections, Cost Structure, and Path to Profitability

**Version:** 1.0  
**Model Period:** 24 months (MVP → V1 → Scale)

---

## 1. Revenue Model Overview

GramMate captures value through **five primary channels**, each with distinct unit economics:

```
Total Annual Revenue = Creator Payouts (20%) + Ads (30%) + Wallet Fees (1.5%) + Premium Tiers (recurring) + Brand Marketplace (15%)
```

## 2. Detailed Revenue Projections (Monthly)

### 2.1 Viewer Reward Payouts (Platform's 20% Cut)

**Mechanics:**
- Platform pays viewers micro-rewards for engagement
- Platform deducts 20% as revenue
- Example: $100 in viewer rewards paid out → $20 platform revenue

**Monthly projections:**

| Month | MAU | AVG Session (min) | Sessions/user/mo | Views/session | Reward/view | Total Viewer Rewards | Platform 20% |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| M3 | 50K | 10 | 15 | 3 | $0.012 | $27K | **$5.4K** |
| M6 | 300K | 12 | 18 | 4 | $0.013 | $225K | **$45K** |
| M12 | 3M | 15 | 20 | 5 | $0.014 | $2.1M | **$420K** |
| M18 | 15M | 18 | 22 | 6 | $0.015 | $11.9M | **$2.38M** |
| M24 | 50M | 20 | 25 | 7 | $0.015 | $43.75M | **$8.75M** |

**Assumptions:**
- TAM grows 10x every 6 months (viral growth)
- Session length increases as product matures (more engaging feed)
- Per-view reward stable (~$0.012–$0.015) as viewer base diversifies globally

### 2.2 Ad Revenue (30% Platform Cut)

**Mechanics:**
- Advertisers buy impressions at CPM (cost per thousand impressions)
- Creators earn 80% of ad revenue
- Platform (GramMate) keeps 20% of gross ad spend

**Assumptions:**
- Ad inventory: 30% of videos served with ads (pre-roll, mid-roll)
- Average CPM: $2–$5 (depends on audience geography, content category)
- Click-through rate: 2–3%

**Monthly projections:**

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| M3 | 50K | 0.05B | 15M | $1.50 | $22.5K | **$4.5K** |
| M6 | 300K | 0.45B | 135M | $2.00 | $270K | **$54K** |

**Assumptions:**
- CPM increases over time as audience quality improves (more US/Western viewers)

### 2.5 Brand Marketplace (15% Facilitation Fee)

**Mechanics:**
- Creators + brands negotiate sponsored content deals directly on platform
- Platform takes 15% of brand deal value
- Example: $1,000 branded video → platform earns $150

**Adoption timeline:**
- M6–9: Limited marketplace (manual partnerships, testing)
- M10+: Full self-serve marketplace (advertiser + creator discovery)

**Projections:**

**Growth driver:** As creator base scales to 100K+ and advertiser awareness grows, brand deal volume accelerates.

## 4. Cost Structure

### 4.1 COGS (Cost of Goods Sold)

**Stripe processing fees:**
- Creator ACH payouts: 1.25% + $0.25 per transaction → ~1.5% effective
- Viewer withdrawals: Similar, ~1.5%
- Total payout volume M24: $500M+ → COGS ~$7.5M/year

**Bandwidth & storage:**
- S3 storage: $0.023 per GB (assume 50TB of videos)
- CloudFront bandwidth: $0.085 per GB (assume 100PB monthly outbound)
- Monthly: 100PB × $0.085 = $8.5M/month at scale
- Optimized with compression: ~$2M/month at M24

**Database costs (managed services):**
- PostgreSQL RDS: Multi-AZ, 8 vCPU, 500 GB storage → ~$3K/month
- ElastiCache Redis: cluster, ~$1K/month
- Snowflake (data warehouse): Usage-based, ~$10K/month at M24

**Total COGS breakdown (M24):**
- Stripe/payments: $7.5M/year → $625K/month
- Bandwidth: $2M/month
- Database: $14K/month
- **Total COGS: ~$2.64M/month (~5.6% of revenue)**

**Gross margin: 94.4%**

## 5. Unit Economics

### 5.1 Viewer Unit Economics

**Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC - Viewer):**
- Paid ads: $1–$2 per signup (TikTok, YouTube, Facebook)
- Organic/referral: $0.50 per signup (viral coefficient)
- Blended: **~$0.75 CAC at scale**

**Lifetime Value (LTV - Viewer):**
- Average user tenure: 2 years
- Monthly engagement: 15 sessions × $0.50/session = $7.50/month
- Monthly active period: 12 months/year × 2 years = 24 months active
- Total earnings: $7.50 × 24 = $180 retained on platform (users rarely fully cash out)
- Platform revenue (20% of $180): **$36 LTV per viewer**

**LTV/CAC ratio:** $36 / $0.75 = **48x** (extremely healthy; >3x is good)

**Payback period:** 3–6 months (first withdrawal typically triggers retention inflection)

---

### 5.2 Creator Unit Economics

**CAC (Creator):**
- Influencer partnerships (guaranteed minimums): $5–$10 per creator acquired
- Organic (from viewers discovering platform): $2–$3
- Blended: **~$6 CAC**

**LTV (Creator):**
- Average creator tenure: 3 years (high competition, churn)
- Monthly earnings (median): $500 (bottom 50%), top 10% earn $8K+
- Platform 20% cut: Assume $100/month average per creator
- Total LTV: $100 × 36 months = **$3,600 LTV**  

**LTV/CAC ratio:** $3,600 / $6 = **600x** (exceptional; indicates creators are extremely high-value)

**Payback period:** 2–3 months (creators see payout within 2 weeks; high commitment)

## 9. Key Financial Assumptions (Summary)

**User Growth:**
- M6: 300K MAU
- M12: 3M MAU
- M24: 50M MAU
- Growth driver: viral referrals (30–40%), paid ads (40–50%), organic (10–20%)

**Monetization:**
- Viewer reward: $0.012–$0.015 per view (stable)
- Ad CPM: $2–$3 (improves as audience quality increases)
- Creator 20% cut of payout volume (fixed economics)

**Cost Structure:**
- Payment processor fees: ~1.5% of payouts
- Bandwidth: Scales with content volume; ~4–5% of revenue at scale
- Salaries: Scales with team growth; ~2–3% of revenue at M24
- Marketing: Front-loaded; decreases as % of revenue (from 30% M1–6 → 2% M24)

**Exit multiple:** 3–5x revenue (for high-growth social platform) = $140B–$235B at $50M revenue (M24)
- Conservative: $2B–$3B valuation at M24 (IPO candidate)
